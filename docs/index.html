OpenBCDR

Check a business continuity or disaster recovery plan against public standards. Rule checks find broken recovery-time dependencies, stale contacts, missing owners and untested versions. Claude then assesses coverage against FFIEC BCM (2019), FINRA Rule 4370 and NIST SP 800-34, and every claim of coverage must quote the plan word for word or it is marked unverified.

Decision support only. OpenBCDR does not certify compliance or replace a qualified BC/DR professional. A person reviews every finding before anyone acts on it. Never paste or upload confidential or regulated information you are not allowed to share.

Try it in your browser

Runs inside Claude on your own account. No install and no API key. Sign in to Claude, paste a sanitized plan, and get the full report. Your plan is not stored by the page.

What it checks

  • Recovery objectives that depend on a system that recovers later than they need it
  • Critical systems with no recovery time or recovery point objective
  • Contacts not verified within 90 days, roles with no backup, contacts with no phone or email
  • Procedures with placeholders like TBD, missing steps or owners, or references to retired systems
  • Critical vendors with no recovery expectation, escalation path or SLA
  • Plans, BIAs and tests that are out of date, and a current version that has never been tested
  • Coverage of up to 73 requirements across the three standards, each finding backed by a verified quote

Method: coverage judged by model against the standards index; every
coverage claim quote-verified against plan source text. Coherence
findings are computed, not inferred. All findings require risk-owner
disposition before any plan change.
