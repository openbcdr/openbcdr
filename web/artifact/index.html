OpenBCDR · open-source BC/DR plan review

Check a recovery plan against public standards

Paste a business continuity or disaster recovery plan. Rule checks catch broken recovery-time dependencies, stale contacts, missing owners and untested versions. Claude then assesses coverage against FFIEC BCM (2019), FINRA Rule 4370 and NIST SP 800-34, and every claim of coverage must quote your plan word for word.

Runs on your own Claude account. No API key. The analysis uses your Claude usage, and Claude asks you to allow it the first time. Your plan is not stored by this page. Decision support only. This does not certify compliance or replace a qualified BC/DR professional. A person reviews every finding before anyone acts on it.

Findings

OpenBCDR is Apache-2.0 open source: github.com/openbcdr/openbcdr. This page runs the same analysis core as the command-line tool, ported to the browser and checked against it. The standards index has not yet been confirmed record by record against its primary sources, so the report withholds an overall score and marks itself not examiner-ready. The findings are still useful as a review checklist. The safety screen only catches generic patterns. It cannot recognize your organization's names or systems, so sanitize before you paste.
